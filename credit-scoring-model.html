About this model. The training data is synthetically generated in-browser to follow realistic lending relationships (higher income and longer history help; high utilization, high debt-to-income, and recent late payments or hard inquiries hurt). It is a teaching artifact, not a production underwriting system, and it is not financial or lending advice — real credit decisions rely on regulated bureau data, fair-lending compliance, and validated models.
